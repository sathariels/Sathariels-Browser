# Sidebar icons

X, Google, and Canvas are bundled SVG marks from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), retrieved October 28, 2026. The project distributes these vectors under CC0; brand marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. Icons identify links to those services and do not imply affiliation. No runtime icon requests are made.

Sources: `icons/x.svg`, `icons/google.svg`, and `icons/canvas.svg` on the Simple Icons develop branch. Original geometry is preserved, with white, Google blue, and Canvas red fills for the dark rail. Native browser controls use SF Symbols and the existing blue theme.
